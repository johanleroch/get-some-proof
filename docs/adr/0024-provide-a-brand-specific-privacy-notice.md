# Provide a brand-specific privacy notice

Every Collection Form links to a hosted privacy notice that identifies the Brand, the testimonial data collected, the intended publication, the private use of email, the service providers involved, and the withdrawal route. The Owner must provide a privacy contact and may replace the hosted notice with its own policy URL; the default legal text requires review before launch, avoiding both a missing notice and the false implication that Get Some Proof alone controls the Brand's publication choices.
