# Launch with one monthly Stripe plan

The MVP sells one Pro subscription at EUR 29 per month through Stripe Checkout and delegates payment-method and subscription management to Stripe Customer Portal. It launches without a trial, annual billing, coupons, or custom proration rules, accepting Stripe as the initial billing provider to keep entitlement transitions auditable and avoid multiple commercial paths before demand is validated. Pro is granted only when a signature-verified synchronized Subscription matches both the Customer and the exact Price mapping persisted server-side during Checkout; an Organization metadata match or Checkout return URL alone is insufficient.
