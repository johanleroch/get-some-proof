# Preserve the submitter's words

An Owner may correct submitter identity metadata, choose which optional identity fields appear publicly, and publish, archive, mark as Spam, or permanently delete a Testimonial, but may not edit its testimonial text. The original Submitter may instead create a Submission Revision through their private management link; this preserves authenticity and the relationship between submitted content and Publication Consent rather than turning Get Some Proof into an unattributed copy editor.
