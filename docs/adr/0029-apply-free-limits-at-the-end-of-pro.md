# Apply Free publication limits at the end of Pro

An Owner scheduling Pro cancellation may preselect the two videos and thirteen texts that will remain Published, with reminders seven days and one day before the paid period ends. Without a complete selection, the most recently Published eligible Testimonials remain public; excess texts become Archived, while excess video assets enter their 30-day downloadable retention window before source deletion. This makes the downgrade deterministic without requiring the Owner to be online at the transition.
