# Let submitters revise their own proof

The confirmation email contains a revocable Submission Management Link that remains usable until deletion and lets the original Submitter edit their text and public identity fields, replace their video, or withdraw consent without an account; requesting a replacement link invalidates the former token, and email itself cannot be changed through the link. Work in progress leaves any published version untouched, but confirming a revision requires renewed Publication Consent, removes the prior version from public surfaces, and returns the Testimonial to Pending for Owner review. A video replacement temporarily preserves the old asset, commits only after the new asset is Ready, then deletes the old Mux media; revisions consume neither another Free Collection Credit nor another Pro Video Slot.
