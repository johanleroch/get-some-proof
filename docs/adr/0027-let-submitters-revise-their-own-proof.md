# Let submitters revise their own proof

The confirmation email contains a revocable Submission Management Link that remains usable until deletion and lets the original Submitter edit their text and public identity fields, replace their video, or withdraw consent without an account; email itself cannot be changed through the link. Replacement-link requests are rate limited, non-enumerating, and durably delivered: concurrent requests for the same Brand and email collapse into one email, the former token remains active through provider failure, and successful delivery atomically activates every replacement link for that email while invalidating the former tokens. The outbox stores only HMAC seeds and token hashes, never a usable bearer token, and deletes them after success, terminal failure, withdrawal, or permanent deletion. Work in progress leaves any published version untouched, but confirming a revision requires renewed Publication Consent, removes the prior version from public surfaces, and returns the Testimonial to Pending for Owner review. A video replacement temporarily preserves the old asset, commits only after the new asset is Ready, then deletes the old Mux media; a permanent-deletion tombstone blocks late replacement reservations, attachments, and confirmations. Revisions consume neither another Free Collection Credit nor another Pro Video Slot.
