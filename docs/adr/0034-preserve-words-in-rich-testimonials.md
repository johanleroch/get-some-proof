# Preserve words while adding emphasis and images

Text Testimonials retain their canonical plain text and may carry a bounded document of paragraphs and highlighted leaves. Owners may change emphasis only: the server rejects formatting whose plain text differs from the submitted text. Submitters may change the content and up to three accompanying raster images through a Submission Revision with fresh Publication Consent; this removes the old Public Projection and returns the Testimonial to Pending.

Images are attachments below the quote, not arbitrary editor nodes or external URLs. Upload reservations bind each image to a Brand and a private submission identifier or authorized management link. Attached images participate in expiry, withdrawal, Spam and Workspace deletion cleanup. Public surfaces receive the same safe projection and rendering. Existing plain-text Testimonials require no migration.

This extends ADR 0020 without authorizing Owners to rewrite a Submitter's words. A small portable document is intentionally separate from Plate's full plugin schema: unsupported formatting, links, HTML and inline image layouts cannot enter published content.
